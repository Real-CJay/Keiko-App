Session note

Stop rules

  • Sharp pain, a pinch or a clunk in the hip — stop that rep, drop the height. Don't finish the set.
  • Any lower back pain — end the session, write it in the note, tell your physio.
  • Running distance rises about 10% a week. Never jump.
  • No bouncing, no partner-forced stretches, no straight-leg toe touches, no sit-ups.
  • Nothing above chest height until the hip is cleared.
  • Sunday is rest. Six working days beat seven every time.

Or add to Google Calendar

One tap per day. Opens Google Calendar with a weekly repeating event already filled in — no file, works from either version of the app.

Google uses your calendar's default reminder, usually 30 minutes before. To be alerted at the exact time, open Google Calendar settings and set the default notification to 0 minutes.

Appearance

Backup

This app stores everything on this device only. Clearing your browser data erases it. Export a backup now and then.

Install on your phone

Android / Brave / Chrome: open the site, tap the ⋮ menu, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
iPhone: open in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
Desktop: look for the install icon at the right of the address bar.

Once installed it opens full screen with its own icon and works with no internet. If you are signed in, anything you tick offline syncs the next time you have a connection.

About this plan

Built around your physio's programme, never instead of it — their set goes before and after every session, 7-second holds as given. If anything here contradicts what they told you, theirs wins. Tell them you have added two running days.
